## ✨ What you'll find

**A clock that lives with you.**  
It doesn't show your system time by asking it once. It reads every second, every movement, every drift — and moves with it, smoothly, the way a real clock does.

**Light that follows the sun.**  
The room is brighter in the morning, warmer at midday, and dark at night. Shadows fall to the left in the morning and to the right in the afternoon, just like a window you could stand in front of.

**The sound of ticking.**  
Click once anywhere on the page and the clock wakes up. From then on, every second lands with a soft tick. On the hour, a chime. At midnight, something softer still.

**Uptime, honestly kept.**  
The counter under the clock starts from a fixed date and never forgets. It's not a score, not a streak — just a small honest number.

**Nothing asks anything of you.**  
No sign-up, no newsletter, no pop-ups. The page exists to be looked at. That's all.
